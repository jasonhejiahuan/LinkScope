# LinkScope Lite Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Applies to: LinkScope Lite 2.0.0 for macOS

Developer: JASON Studio

LinkScope Lite helps you inspect accessories connected to your Mac. The app processes accessory information on your Mac and does not send your observations, device identifiers, saved history, or dashboard content to JASON Studio or third-party analytics services. It has no account system, advertising, tracking SDK, or automatic cloud synchronization.

## Information used on your Mac

Depending on your hardware, macOS, and permissions, the app reads information exposed by macOS about accessories and the system. This can include device names, Bluetooth addresses, serial numbers and other hardware identifiers; connection and pairing state; available signal strength and battery information; audio endpoint names and routing; hardware properties; your Mac's name and operating-system version; and power, thermal, sleep, and wake state.

The app uses this information to display devices and parameters, associate observations with devices, show changes over time, and run diagnostics you initiate. It also processes your dashboard layouts, rules, diagnostic session names, saved snapshots, imported files, and app preferences. These remain local unless you choose to export or share them.

LinkScope Lite does not record microphone audio, keystrokes, pointer movements, or game-controller gameplay input. It does not read passwords or credentials belonging to other apps.

## Local storage and protection

Live observations can work in memory before saved history is configured. Enabling saved history allows the app to store its own master key in the macOS Keychain and save data locally. It uses Apple's CryptoKit AES-256-GCM to encrypt sensitive serialized payloads, HKDF-SHA256 to derive separate keys, and HMAC-SHA256 for local identifier matching.

This protection covers payloads such as raw observation values, device identifiers, and dashboard content. It is not encryption of the entire SQLite database. Some query metadata—including timestamps, local record identifiers, parameter paths, status information, and saved snapshot names—and app preferences are not encrypted by LinkScope. The master key is configured as a device-only Keychain item; LinkScope does not synchronize it through iCloud. Your own backups and system security settings are separate from these protections.

## Files you import or export

You choose the files the app may import and the destination of exports using macOS file dialogs. Snapshot JSON, diagnostic CSV, and dashboard JSON exports are ordinary, unencrypted files. They may contain device names, identifiers, raw values, and text or configuration you supplied. Dashboard documents can also preserve additional fields from imported files.

Exporting does not send a file to JASON Studio. Review its contents before sharing. Saving to a synchronized folder, using another app to share it, or sending it to support may move it off your Mac under the relevant service's terms and privacy practices. LinkScope does not automatically redact exports or control copies you share.

## Retention and deletion

Saved history is retained without a time limit by default. In Settings > Storage, you may select 30, 90, or 365 days, preview older observations, and explicitly confirm their deletion. Selecting a period alone does not start automatic cleanup.

That operation deletes older observation records only. It does not delete copies inside saved snapshots, device identity records, timeline entries, diagnostic sessions, rules, preferences, Keychain keys, or exported files. Dashboards and rules can be deleted individually where their delete controls are available.

Version 2.0.0 does not include a single control to erase all local app data and its Keychain key. Uninstalling the app is not a guarantee that every local record, key, backup, or export is removed. Contact support for help with your data-management needs. Because we do not hold your local database, we cannot retrieve or remotely erase it. Manage exported files and backup copies in the locations or services you chose.

## Your choices and permissions

You can review the app's permission status in Settings > Permissions. Bluetooth access can be revoked in macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth. Notification permission can be changed in System Settings > Notifications; notifications generated by app rules are local notifications. Availability of related features changes with these permissions.

You may decline saved-history setup and use available live features without configuring persistent history. Quitting LinkScope Lite stops new observations by the app. Disabling a permission does not delete information already saved. Shortcuts run at your direction and can return information to the shortcut you invoke.

## Website visits and support

Opening our website or privacy-policy link makes a web request through your browser. Website infrastructure may process technical request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. The app's local-processing statement is not a promise that website servers keep no access logs.

If you contact JASON Studio, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to provide. We use them to respond to your request and resolve the issue, retaining them as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Hosting and communication providers may process those requests to deliver their services. We do not use support correspondence for advertising or sell it. You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you supplied through support, subject to applicable requirements. A public issue tracker can make your message and attachments visible to others. Do not post your database, full exports, or sensitive identifiers in a public issue.

Apple may separately handle App Store transactions and system diagnostics under Apple's policies and your system choices.

## Contact and updates

For privacy questions or support-data requests, contact JASON Studio through [LinkScope Support](https://apps.jasonstu.cc/linkscope/support).

We will publish changes at [LinkScope Privacy](https://apps.jasonstu.cc/linkscope/privacy) and update the effective date. If a future version changes how the app handles information, its policy and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.
